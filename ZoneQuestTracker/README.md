# ZoneQuestTracker

ZoneQuestTracker groups watched quests by zone in the right-side Objective Tracker in **World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 (Camelot)**. Click a zone header to toggle that zone or left-click a quest title to focus the quest and open Blizzard’s map at its location. Click the quest pin or its entry in Blizzard’s quest log to open the details. Right-click a quest title for **Track** or **Untrack**, **Share**, and **Abandon**. In a group, hover over a quest title to see your party members’ progress on it. The Forever server doesn’t share party quest progress, so a party member’s progress appears only if they also run ZoneQuestTracker 1.0.13 or later. Click the circle beside a quest to focus it without opening the map, or click the focused circle again to clear the focus. The circle follows Blizzard’s Quest Points of Interest setting. If the section fills the available tracker height, use the mouse wheel over the list.

## Install

1. Exit World of Warcraft and copy the entire `ZoneQuestTracker` folder into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`.
2. Check that `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/ZoneQuestTracker/ZoneQuestTracker.toc` exists.
3. Start Forever and enable **ZoneQuestTracker** in the character selection screen’s **AddOns** list. If ForeverCore’s **Forever quest list** is enabled, turn it off to avoid displaying a second quest list.

## Tracker behavior

The addon uses the quest log’s category, then the quest’s destination zone, then **Other**. Each watched quest has one row even if it sends you to several zones. Zone collapse state is saved per character. You can expand, collapse, and scroll zones during combat. Objective counts and ready-for-turn-in status update as you play. Once a quest is complete, its objectives are replaced by a single **Ready for turn-in** line below the title, marked with a green check. A quest item that Blizzard still offers moves to that line. Completed quests are listed first in their zone; other quests keep their tracked order. Each zone header shows how many quests are ready, for example **Tanaris (4, 2 ready)**, including while the zone is collapsed. During combat the count updates right away, and a quest that completes moves to the top of its zone when combat ends. Changes to which quests appear and where item buttons are placed apply after combat.

Out of combat, a quest title focuses the quest and opens Blizzard’s map at its location. During combat, a title click opens a closed map immediately through a secure hardware action and moves the yellow circle to the quest. Blizzard’s map stays on its current zone until combat ends. If the map is still open then, it moves to the last quest you clicked and highlights it. Otherwise, the quest becomes Blizzard’s focused quest without reopening the map. The addon does not move the map during combat because Blizzard rejects quest-log updates triggered by addons in combat with **Interface action failed because of an AddOn**. Select the quest pin in the map to view its details. Quest menu actions are available out of combat. **Share** requires a group and a shareable quest. **Abandon** opens Blizzard’s confirmation dialog. You can track a quest again from Blizzard’s quest log.

The grouped display follows Blizzard’s quest and campaign watch sections. Blizzard continues to lay out its tracker while ZoneQuestTracker fades the stock quest rows out of combat. Edit Mode shows the stock tracker so you can adjust it there.

When Blizzard provides a quest item, its icon appears to the right of the first objective or status line. Hover over an active shortcut for the tooltip. An item button already in place when combat begins stays clickable while its quest and position match. Newly shown or moved item icons stay gray until their buttons can be placed after combat. The icon also turns gray when the client reports the item is unavailable, on cooldown, or out of range. The game still checks quest-specific conditions when you use the item, such as whether the target is wounded enough for the Phantasmal Blade.

The grouped display supports mouse controls. Blizzard’s auto-quest popups and gamepad navigation for quest rows are unavailable in this display.

## In-game test checklist

- [ ] Track quests from two categories, such as The Barrens and Stonetalon Mountains. Confirm the two headers show the right counts and a multi-zone quest appears only once, under its quest-log category.
- [ ] Collapse one zone and confirm its header, count, and `+` stay visible while the other zone stays open. Reverse the two states and confirm they remain independent.
- [ ] Progress a quest while still in combat and confirm its visible count updates. Repeat with a closed zone. Check that the zone stays closed, then expand it during combat to see the new progress. Confirm that when the quest is complete, only the **Ready for turn-in** line remains below its title. Check that the zone header shows the ready count, for example **(2, 1 ready)**, both expanded and collapsed. During combat, confirm the count updates and the completed quest stays in place, then moves to the top of its zone after combat. With two completed quests in one zone, confirm they keep their tracked order.
- [ ] Click quest titles from two zones with the map closed and already open. Confirm the yellow focus marker follows each click, Blizzard’s map shows the quest location, and clicking its pin opens the details. Enter combat with the addon-opened map visible and check for protected-action errors while making quest progress. In combat, expand a closed zone, scroll to its quests, and click a title. Test with the map open and closed. Confirm the map opens immediately with no **Interface action failed** message, the yellow circle moves immediately, and the map stays open when selecting another quest. Verify that when combat ends the open map moves to the last clicked quest. Click headers, objectives, and spaces between quests to check that they preserve the map state.
- [ ] Right-click a quest title and check that only the menu opens. Untrack the quest, then track it again from Blizzard’s quest log. Check that the count and rows update, including when removing the last quest in a zone and then restoring it. Verify **Share** is disabled solo and enabled for a shareable quest in a group. In a group, hover over a quest title with the map closed and open, and in combat. Confirm the tooltip shows each party member’s progress. Solo, confirm no tooltip appears. Open **Abandon** and cancel Blizzard’s confirmation. Verify menu actions stay disabled in combat.
- [ ] With Quest Points of Interest enabled, click the circles beside two watched quests in turn and check that focus and the highlighted circle follow the selection. Click the focused circle again to clear it. Turn the setting off and on to check that the circles hide and return.
- [ ] Focus the first quest in a zone and check that its circle and glow clear the zone header. Track a quest with a usable item and check its right-side tooltip. For Tempered Expectations, weaken eligible Shen’dralas wildlife, move into melee range, and left-click the Phantasmal Blade while still in combat. Verify the quest counter increases and the icon grays during cooldown, then returns to color when usable. In combat, collapse the item’s zone or scroll its icon out of view and check that the old position cannot use the item. Verify displaced and newly visible shortcuts bind after combat. Check that the icon disappears when Blizzard stops offering the item.
- [ ] Leave one zone closed and the other open, run `/reload`, and check both states. With a quest item on the tracker, check for blocked-action errors on reload. Repeat after logging out and in. Check that another character can set its own states.
- [ ] With quests as the only tracker content, use Blizzard’s main tracker minimize button and expand it again. Then track an achievement or another nonquest objective to check that it still uses its own section.
- [ ] With the tracker crowded, scroll over the grouped list and check that the last quest is reachable. Enter and exit combat and check for Lua errors or blocked-action messages.
- [ ] With several zones expanded and enough quests to fill the tracker, exit WoW and log in again. Verify the tracker uses the available height and the last quest is reachable without `/reload`. Repeat after changing the tracker height in Edit Mode or changing the UI scale.
- [ ] Track a quest that has a clickable item and progress a quest during combat. Confirm the tracker can update without a blocked `QuestObjectiveTracker:SetHeight()` action and that the grouped rows resume when combat ends. Switch stance or form and check for blocked actions during the action-bar update. Open Edit Mode out of combat, move the tracker, then exit and confirm the grouped list follows it.

## Compatibility review

The original quest and tracker contracts were checked against build **1.60.1.70888**. Item use and the tracker’s layout order were checked against build **1.60.1.70938**. [Quest API](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/4cd0ab7/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/QuestLogDocumentation.lua) · [Tracker container](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_ObjectiveTracker/Blizzard_ObjectiveTrackerContainer.lua#L59-L123) · [Quest item lookup](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_FrameXMLUtil/Mainline/QuestUtils.lua#L337-L343) · [Click-to-use template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_FrameXML/SecureTemplates.xml#L3-L8)

Offline Lua 5.1 checks passed for quest grouping and fallback, deduplication, independent collapse, progress and turn-in status during combat, map clicks, watch changes, simulated reload and character isolation, secure item-button placement, tracker minimization, scrolling, and recovery to Blizzard’s quest display on error.

## Local 1.0.12 verification

In Forever **1.60.1.70959**, title clicks focused quests and opened the correct map in Tanaris and Feralas. Live checks also covered the quest menu, untracking and re-tracking, Share availability in a party and solo, cancellation of Blizzard’s Abandon confirmation, independent collapse, scrolling, item tooltip placement, and the gold map-pin logo under **AddOns → Quests**. The player verified live progress for More Wastewander Justice, combat zone expansion, and deferred map opening. The map-only build passed a fight with the map open and quest progress updating, with no reported protected-action error.

An earlier integrated build triggered **Interface action failed because of an AddOn** during combat. A captured call stack traced it to the addon calling `WorldMapFrame:SetMapID` in combat. Blizzard then rebuilt its quest log as part of the addon’s action, and its gamepad SmartNavigation check rejected that rebuild in combat. It also left the reused quest-log buttons in a state that failed again on later quest updates while the map stayed open. The addon no longer changes Blizzard’s map, quest log, or focused quest during combat. With only ZoneQuestTracker enabled, the player opened the map from a closed state in combat with and without earlier out-of-combat title clicks, and no message appeared. With OpenMap enabled, the same message still came from Blizzard’s map code, where OpenMap adds a map data provider and changes the map.

The player also confirmed that completed quests show only **Ready for turn-in** below their titles. Item use on an eligible target, cooldown gray-to-color recovery, and a second-character persistence check remain unverified in this build. The full checklist above is available for additional testing.
